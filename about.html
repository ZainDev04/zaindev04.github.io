The short version

Eight weeks, raw rows to a deployed paper.

I spent eight weeks taking a real production search dataset from raw rows to a deployed research paper: data contract, frozen baseline, model, validation, write-up.

The hardest part wasn’t any single technical wall. It was finishing, alongside a full course load, without quietly dropping the tests I’d promised myself I’d run.

The paper reports five archetypes instead of six because one of them didn’t survive those tests.

I work in the open. Every number in the paper is regenerated by a committed notebook, and the receipts are JSON files in the repo, so nothing rests on my word.
